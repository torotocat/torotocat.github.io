2021-10-31 · First, climate change triggers cha…

First, climate change triggers changes in the water cycle. Due to the increase of urban population density, anthropogenic heat emission, air pollution and heat island effect lead to further changes in water cycle, thus causing changes in urban microclimate. Second, urban development and construction caused by the natural stagnation of the ground, rainwater storage capacity reduced. Third, the drainage capacity of drainage pipes and canals and other waterlogging facilities is insufficient. Fourth, human factors. Long-term over-exploitation of groundwater leads to land subsidence; Poor construction management causes construction waste or household garbage to block pipes; Pump station failure; Landscape avenue reconstruction cover or manhole cover destruction.
